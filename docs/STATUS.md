# Implementation status

Legend: **Done** = working end to end with tests · **Partial** = usable but incomplete ·
**Missing** = not started. A route, menu item or placeholder does not count as done.

| Module | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| A. Company & configuration | Done | Multi-company, switch, settings, GSTIN/PAN validation, FY + monthly period locks, users/roles per company, custom roles. |
| B. Accounting engine | Done (core) | Groups, ledgers, cost centres, voucher types/numbering, 8 voucher classes, bill-wise, draft/approval/post/cancel/reverse, revisions. Recurring vouchers (weekly to yearly, daily scheduler, catch-up, failure log). Missing: opening bill-wise refs, recurring invoices, multi-currency. |
| C. Sales | Partial | Done: customers, sales invoices with items and GST, credit notes against invoices, receivables with ageing, invoice print and PDF, credit-limit warning. Stock items on invoice lines. Sales orders with invoices against them (part delivery), close/cancel and a pending orders report. Delivery notes that move stock, with invoices made from them and a delivered-not-invoiced report. Quotations with validity and conversion to sales orders. Revisions on quotations and orders: each change keeps the earlier version (Rev 0, 1, …) with a reason, who and when, and a list of what changed (10t). Advances against sales and purchase orders (receipt or payment with an Advance bill), adjusted against the order's invoices by a settling journal (10u). Missing: automatic adjustment when the invoice is posted. |
| D. Purchases | Partial | Done: suppliers, purchase bills (duplicate supplier bill check), debit notes, payables with ageing. Stock items on bill lines. Purchase orders with bills against them, close/cancel and a pending orders report. Goods receipt notes with bills made from them and a received-not-billed report. GRNI provision: a dated journal booking unbilled GRN value to purchases against a Goods Received Not Invoiced liability, reversed the next day (10s). Advances paid against purchase orders and adjusted against their bills (10u). Missing: automatic month-end GRNI. |
| E. GST | Partial | CGST/SGST/IGST on invoices from place of supply and registration type; GSTR-1 working papers (B2B, B2CL, B2CS, exports, CDNR/CDNUR, HSN, documents) with CSV; GSTR-3B with ITC and set-off; purchase register; cess and reverse charge on invoices (10l). GSTR-1 JSON for the portal (10p). Missing: GSTR-1 amendments and GSTR-3B JSON, GSTR-2B matching, specific-rate cess, e-invoice, e-way bill. |
| F. Inventory | Partial | Done: units, stock groups and categories, godowns, stock items with opening stock, stock on invoices and returns, transfers and adjustments (stock journals), weighted average valuation, closing stock in P&L and balance sheet, stock summary, item movement ledger, reorder status, negative-stock warning. Batch numbers with expiry and serial numbers on invoices, returns, stock journals and opening stock, with batch/expiry and serial reports. Delivery notes and GRNs move stock. FIFO or average costing per item; bill of materials and manufacture (10n). Stock ageing by receipt date with custom day slabs, godown and group filters (10q). Missing: goods in transit, by-products and scrap, batches on import. |
| G. Banking | Partial | Bank ledgers with bank details, bank book, bank reconciliation with bank dates, CSV, OFX / QFX, MT940 and CAMT.053 statement import with auto/manual matching and duplicate protection for OFX, MT940 and CAMT.053 (10o, 10v). Cheque register (issued/received, cleared/pending/stale/post-dated), cheque books with spoilt leaves, cheque printing with per-book layout. Missing: batch cheque printing, positive pay. |
| H. Reports | Partial | Done: ledger, cash/bank book, day book, trial balance, group summary, P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, stock summary, dashboard, CSV. Receivables/payables with ageing. GST returns. CSV, Excel and PDF export on every report including P&L and balance sheet. Ratio analysis (12 ratios with principal groups, against last year). Period comparison of P&L and balance sheet by month, quarter, year or against last year, with change and totals. Budgets for groups and ledgers (optionally per cost centre, on transactions or closing balance) with a variance report, pro-rata budget to date, over-budget flags and copy to next year. Funds flow statement (long-term sources and applications, working capital changes, self-check). Missing: monthly budget phasing. |
| I. Payroll | Partial | Done: employees, pay heads, salary structures, paid days, PF/ESI/professional tax/TDS, pay sheet, payslips, posting a salary journal, cancellation; attendance and leave registers, overtime, loans and advances recovered through payroll (10m). Salary payment voucher, bank transfer list, PF ECR v2 text file and ESI monthly contribution file, each listing employees left out for missing details (10r). Missing: attendance import, leave carry-forward, Form 16, bank-specific upload formats, PF arrears. |
| J. Transport | Partial | Done: vehicles, drivers with advance ledgers, routes, trips, expenses posting vouchers, freight bills as sales invoices, trip/vehicle/route/customer/driver reports. Missing: LR printing, e-way bill numbers, multi-trip bills, document expiry reminders, tyre tracking. |
| K. Security & audit | Done (core) | Permissions per company, audit trail with old/new values, login/logout/failed login logs, maker-checker. Two-factor sign-in with an authenticator app (QR set-up, recovery codes, no code reuse, rate limit), and a company setting that requires it for every member. Missing: IP restrictions, SMS/email codes. |
| L. Data management | Partial | Done: company backups with checksums, verify, restore with safety backup, scheduled backups, integrity checks; CSV/Excel import of ledgers, parties, stock items and vouchers with preview, error report and duplicate detection; CSV/Excel export of masters, vouchers and every report. Missing: invoice import, Tally XML, off-site/encrypted backups, API sync. |
| M. Dashboard & usability | Partial | Responsive layout, dashboard figures, Alt-key shortcuts. Global search across ledgers, items, batches/serials, invoices, vouchers, orders, notes, employees and trips (permission-aware, `/` or Ctrl+K). Missing: search-as-you-type dropdown, configurable shortcuts. |

Testing: run `php artisan test` (SQLite in memory by default). The full suite also passes on MySQL 8.0 (set `DB_CONNECTION=mysql` and the `DB_*` variables), and every migration rolls back cleanly on both. `.github/workflows/tests.yml` runs PHP 8.2 and 8.3 against SQLite and MySQL.

Per-phase details and manual checklists: `docs/phase-*.md`.
