# Phase 3 — Customers, suppliers, invoices and outstanding

## What was built

* **Customers and suppliers** (`/customers`, `/suppliers`). Each party is a ledger under
  Sundry Debtors / Sundry Creditors (or a sub-group) plus a `parties` row with GST
  registration type, GSTIN, PAN, contact person, phone, email, billing and shipping address,
  city, state and PIN. Opening balance, bill-wise flag, credit period and credit limit stay on
  the ledger. GSTIN format, its state code and its embedded PAN are validated; PAN and state
  are filled from the GSTIN when left empty. A party with transactions cannot be deleted
  (mark it inactive). Ledgers created from the ledger screen appear here and get their
  details on first edit.
* **Sales invoices, purchase bills, credit notes (sales returns) and debit notes (purchase
  returns)** (`/invoices?kind=sales|purchase|credit_note|debit_note`).
  * Items: sales/purchase ledger, description, HSN/SAC, quantity (3 decimals), unit, rate,
    discount %, GST rate. HSN and GST rate default from the ledger.
  * All amounts are computed on the server in integer paise
    (`App\Services\Billing\InvoiceCalculator`): gross = qty × rate, discount, taxable value,
    GST per line (CGST = SGST = half, or IGST), optional rounding of the total to the rupee.
  * Tax type: sales compare the place of supply (default: customer's state) with the
    company's state; purchases compare the supplier's state. Purchases from unregistered,
    composition or consumer parties carry no GST. Overseas and SEZ parties are inter-state.
    GST can be turned off for the company.
  * Posting builds a voucher through the voucher engine
    (`App\Services\Billing\InvoiceService`): party Dr/Cr total, item ledgers by taxable
    value, Output/Input CGST/SGST/IGST, Round Off. So numbering, period locks, approval
    (maker-checker), revisions and the audit trail work exactly as for vouchers. The invoice
    number is the voucher number.
  * Bill-wise: a sales invoice opens a bill with its own number and due date (date + party
    credit days unless entered); a purchase bill uses the supplier's invoice number, so the
    same supplier bill cannot be entered twice. A credit/debit note can be made against an
    invoice (Agst Ref, cannot exceed what is pending) or kept as a separate credit.
  * Drafts have no voucher and do not touch the books. Posted documents are altered with a
    reason (new voucher revision) or cancelled with a reason; they are never deleted. An
    invoice cannot be cancelled while a posted credit/debit note refers to it. Vouchers
    generated by an invoice can only be changed through the invoice.
  * Warning (not a block) when a sale takes a customer over the credit limit.
  * **Print** layout (A4): Tax Invoice / Bill of Supply, company and party GSTIN, place of
    supply with state code, items, HSN/rate-wise tax summary, amount in words (Indian
    numbering), bank details (setting), terms and footer.
* **Receivables / Payables** (`/reports/outstanding?type=receivable|payable`): balance per
  party split into ageing buckets (not due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, over 90 days by due date) and
  an on-account remainder (opening balance and unallocated amounts); per-party bill list;
  CSV export.

## Tests

`tests/Feature/Billing/InvoiceTest.php` (18 tests) checks the posted voucher lines for intra-
and inter-state sales, purchases, unregistered suppliers, cash sales, drafts, alteration
with revision, credit notes against invoices, cancellation, ageing as on a date, screens,
permissions and tenant isolation. `PartyTest.php` covers party creation and validation.
`tests/Unit/InvoiceCalculatorTest.php` checks rounding, fractional quantities, amount in words.

## Manual testing checklist

1. Company state Maharashtra. **Customers → New customer**: GSTIN `24ABCDE1234F1Z5` → PAN and
   state (Gujarat) fill in. Enter a Maharashtra state with that GSTIN → error.
2. **Sales Invoices → New**: choose the Gujarat customer → "Inter-state supply: IGST".
   Item 25.5 × 240 at 5%, item 1 × 1,500 with 10% discount at 18% → total 8,019.00.
3. Post → number INV/0001; the voucher view shows party Dr 8,019, Sales Cr 7,470, Output IGST Cr 549.
4. **Print** → Tax Invoice with HSN-wise tax summary and amount in words.
5. Choose a Maharashtra customer → CGST + SGST, half each.
6. **Purchase Bills → New** from a supplier with supplier invoice no. `PS-1`; enter the same
   number again for that supplier → refused.
7. On the invoice, **Credit note** → items copied; reduce quantity, post → the invoice's pending
   amount drops in **Receivables**. Try to cancel the invoice → refused while the note exists.
8. Alter a posted invoice without a reason → refused; with a reason → the voucher history shows
   the previous version.
9. **Receivables**: ageing columns and totals; click a party for its bills; CSV downloads.
10. Sign in as a *Purchases* role member → Sales Invoices and Customers are forbidden.
11. On a phone, invoice items become cards and no page scrolls sideways.

## Known limitations

* Items are ledgers, not stock items; quantities do not move stock yet (Phase 6, inventory).
* No quotations, sales/purchase orders, delivery notes or GRNs yet.
* No cess, TDS/TCS, reverse charge or e-invoice/e-way bill fields yet (Phase 5, GST).
* PDF download uses the browser's print to PDF; server-side PDF is Phase 8.
